# FlowSoundz Radio Social Growth Audit and Program

**Purpose:** Turn the existing FlowSoundz product into a repeatable social discovery and conversion system.

**Brand direction:** Use the existing primary FlowSoundz Radio logo across launch graphics, profile images, link previews, and video end cards until dedicated release artwork is ready.

## Executive Read

FlowSoundz already has the right product story for social: synchronized radio, early discovery, artist spotlights, human review, shareable track pages, listener reactions, and a direct artist submission path. The product is stronger than the current distribution system.

The immediate objective is not to be everywhere. It is to create a measurable loop:

1. A person sees a compelling artist or live-radio moment.
2. They tap a track, radio, or waitlist link.
3. They listen long enough to understand the difference.
4. They share, follow, join the waitlist, or submit music.
5. FlowSoundz uses the response to create the next piece of content.

## Positioning

### Listener promise

**Discover the next sound while the whole room is hearing it too.**

FlowSoundz is live, curated radio for people who want independent music discovery to feel shared instead of endless and anonymous.

### Artist promise

**Get a real introduction, a real listening moment, and useful response data.**

FlowSoundz does not sell guaranteed plays or editorial approval. It provides a professional submission path, review, context, promotion tools, and measurable listener signals.

### Proof points to repeat

- Synchronized radio creates a moment artists can promote.
- Every release gets context, not just a playlist slot.
- Artist-led AI-assisted music can be presented honestly and professionally.
- Curation and paid promotion remain clearly separated.
- The audience response is part of the discovery story.

## Content Program

Use five recurring series. Each post should have one job and one primary call to action.

| Series | Content | CTA | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Now Playing | 10-20 second song moment, artist name, vibe, and exact listen path | Listen live | 3 per week |
| Before They Blow | Short artist introduction with one memorable detail | Discover the artist | 2 per week |
| Radio Room | Live reaction, crowd energy, DJ intro, or visualizer clip | Join the moment | 2 per week |
| Behind the Mix | Artist story, lyric idea, production choice, or honest AI-assisted process note | Read and listen | 1 per week |
| Open Frequency | Submission invitation, founder update, or community question | Submit or join | 1 per week |

### Content rules

- Lead with the song, artist, or live moment in the first two seconds.
- Use the main logo as the closing mark, not as the subject of every post.
- Use captions on every video.
- Name the artist and song on-screen.
- Use one link per post wherever possible.
- Never imply guaranteed placement, guaranteed streams, or paid editorial selection.
- For AI-assisted releases, use plain language such as `artist-led, AI-assisted production` when relevant.
- Recut one strong idea for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Stories rather than making four unrelated ideas.

## Weekly Operating Cadence

### Monday: Select

Choose one focus track, one artist story, one live-radio moment, and one conversion goal. Confirm rights, clean status, artwork/logo treatment, and destination links.

### Tuesday: Publish discovery

Post the artist or track introduction. Reply to every meaningful comment. Save questions for the next content batch.

### Wednesday: Publish live energy

Post a short radio or visualizer moment and invite listeners to hear the synchronized station.

### Thursday: Artist activation

Send the artist a launch kit containing the track link, suggested caption, logo-safe visual, exact air-time information when available, and a request for one personal story.

### Friday: Community conversion

Post the strongest moment of the week and drive one action: listen, join, follow, or submit.

### Sunday: Review

Record reach, link clicks, listeners, completion, shares, follows, waitlist signups, artist submissions, and returning visitors. Keep the winning hook and replace the weakest one.

## Campaign Link Standard

Every social campaign needs a distinct URL with readable attribution:

`https://flowsoundzradio.com/radio?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=artist_focus_YYYYMM&utm_content=now_playing_01`

Use these fields consistently:

- `utm_source`: instagram, tiktok, youtube, x, artist, email, or direct
- `utm_medium`: organic_social, creator_share, paid_social, email, or partner
- `utm_campaign`: launch, artist_focus, discovery_drop, waitlist, or submission
- `utm_content`: the specific post or video identifier

The app should preserve these values for the session and attach them to first-touch and conversion events. This is the highest-value measurement upgrade.

## Funnel And Scorecard

Track the funnel weekly:

1. Reach and video views
2. Profile or link clicks
3. Landing-page sessions
4. Radio starts
5. 60-second listeners and track completions
6. Shares, favorites, follows, and reactions
7. Waitlist signups
8. Artist submissions
9. Returning listeners within seven days
10. Paid artist-tool or promotion purchases

### Initial 30-day validation targets

These are targets, not claims about current traction:

- 30 qualified artist or listener conversations
- 12 short-form videos published
- 4 focused artist features
- 250 unique listeners
- 50 returning listeners
- 50 track shares
- 25 waitlist or account conversions
- 5 complete artist submissions
- 3 artists who voluntarily repost their FlowSoundz feature

The strongest signal is not raw reach. It is the combination of repeat listening, artist reposts, shares per listener, and completed submissions.

## Product Work Required

### P0: Before public social push

- Preserve UTM parameters and referral source through the session.
- Add source and campaign fields to waitlist signups instead of hard-coding `homepage`.
- Add `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` to analytics payloads.
- Build an admin weekly scorecard for the funnel above.
- Add a creator referral link that identifies the artist or campaign without exposing private data.
- Ensure shared track previews use the main FlowSoundz logo when release artwork is unavailable.

### P1: After the first month

- Generate an artist launch kit from the approved release record.
- Show artists their air time, share link, and campaign results in one place.
- Add referral milestones for listener invites and artist reposts.
- Add a lightweight content queue for approved posts and publishing status.

### P2: Only after repeatable demand

- Consider platform posting integrations.
- Add paid campaign reporting and spend controls.
- Add label and manager workspaces.

Do not build automatic posting before the manual content loop shows which messages and artist moments convert.

## Trust And Safety

- Keep the main logo consistent and avoid implying endorsement where the station has only received a submission.
- Use `submitted`, `under review`, `approved`, and `featured` as distinct statuses.
- Keep standard review free and paid promotion clearly labeled.
- Require permission for artist names, likenesses, audio, lyrics, artwork, and reposted clips.
- For AI-assisted releases, retain the Suno source ID, account/tier record, original source materials, and artist attestation.
- Remove or pause a release promptly when a rights holder raises a credible issue.

## Weekly Decision Rule

At the end of each week, keep the content format that produces the best combination of:

- qualified link clicks,
- 60-second listening rate,
- track completion,
- shares per listener,
- artist repost rate,
- and returning listeners.

Drop formats that create views but no meaningful listening or community action after two tests.

## Launch Definition Of Done

Social is launch-ready when:

- the main logo is used consistently;
- four clean FlowSoundz releases have approved metadata and masters;
- every post has an attributed campaign link;
- the weekly scorecard can distinguish social channels;
- one artist can receive and use a launch kit without manual rewriting;
- listeners can move from post to radio to share or waitlist in one clear path;
- the public message is honest about curation, AI assistance, and paid support.
